============
Introduction
============

Jewellery, signs and printed goods are sold in the customer's size or with the customer's text. Such
choices are no stock variants: as articles they would multiply into every combination of sizes and texts.

This extension lets editors define options on a product, or on a single article of it:

*   **Selection lists**, e.g. a ring size or a clasp, with a surcharge per choice.
*   **Free texts**, e.g. an engraving, limited in length and, if needed, to the characters an engraver
    can cut.

The customer chooses them in the add-to-basket form. The same product with other choices becomes a line
of its own; surcharges are part of the line's price and VAT. Basket, checkout review, order mails, invoice
and the order record list the chosen options.

An option or choice marked as personalizing turns the line into goods made to the customer's specification, which
carry no right of withdrawal (Art. 16(c) Directive 2011/83/EU). The checkout then shows the corresponding
notice, and the order item is stored as exempt.
